crawlback Draft 1, 14 Oct 2026

The evidence ledger

Crawlback finds and checks archived copies of web pages. The look is neutral: white cards on a light grey page, near-black text, and one red accent for the element that matters most on each screen. Every capture is shown with its date and provenance trail.

Paper #f7f7f8
Card #ffffff
Ink #141416
Ink 2 #45454d
Accent #c4321a

Type: Instrument Serif for headlines and numbers. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for body copy and labels. Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for URLs, dates and data. The Atkinson fonts stay because Il1 and O0 are easy to tell apart in URLs.
